FAQ

Answers to the
Obvious Questions

What exactly does LinkGuard check?

LinkGuard checks the full redirect chain of any URL — every intermediate hop is exposed. It also inspects HTTP security headers, verifies SSL certificates, and cross-checks the final destination against Google Web Risk and other threat intelligence databases.

How fast are the scan results?

Most scans complete in under 2 seconds. Complex links with multiple redirects may take slightly longer, but you'll always have your result within 5 seconds.

Do I need to create an account?

No. LinkGuard works entirely through Telegram. Your Telegram ID is all that's needed — no email, no password, no personal information required unless you opt in for communications.

How do I pay for a plan?

Payments are handled directly through the Telegram bot interface via Stripe. It's seamless — you never leave Telegram to upgrade or manage your subscription.

What happens if a malicious link is found?

You'll immediately receive a security report flagging the specific threat — phishing attempt, malware host, suspicious redirect, or insecure connection. The report tells you what was found and why the link is considered dangerous.

Is my data private?

Yes. We do not store or sell the URLs you submit. Each scan is processed ephemerally — once the report is returned, the URL is discarded. You can read our full Privacy Policy for details.
